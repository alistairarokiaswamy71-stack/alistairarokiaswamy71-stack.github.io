About

Good research is not just about finding information.

It is about figuring out what actually matters.

My work spans market research, competitive intelligence, market sizing, company research, industry analysis, data validation and strategic research assignments. I enjoy taking scattered information and turning it into a clearer picture of a market, competitor, company or opportunity.

Typical questions I work around include:

  • How large is the market and where is growth coming from?
  • Who are the important competitors and how are they positioned?
  • Which companies, customers, suppliers and market participants matter?
  • What is changing in the industry and why?
  • Which markets, products or opportunities deserve a closer look?
  • What do we know with confidence — and what still needs validation?
