PROJECT 01

Integrated MMF Textile & Apparel Park — West Bengal

Pre-Feasibility · Market Assessment · Commercial Opportunity · Location Strategy

Supported a pre-feasibility and commercial assessment for a proposed integrated Man-Made Fibre (MMF) and apparel textile manufacturing ecosystem in West Bengal. The assignment evaluated market, product, export, location and industrial ecosystem factors relevant to an export-oriented textile manufacturing hub.

Industry Man-Made Fibre · Textiles · Apparel · Technical Textiles
Geography West Bengal · India · Global Export Markets
Project Type Pre-Feasibility & Commercial Assessment
Research Perspective Market · Product · Export · Location · Infrastructure
Research Scope
  • Global and Indian MMF textile market assessment
  • Market segmentation and growth analysis
  • India's textile and apparel export assessment
  • Global competitive benchmarking
  • India vs. major Asian textile-exporting economies
  • Product and SKU opportunity mapping
  • Export-oriented product prioritisation
  • Technical and industrial textile opportunity assessment
  • Performance activewear and synthetic textile analysis
  • Sustainable and EU-aligned textile opportunities
  • Medical and hygiene nonwoven opportunity assessment
  • Raw-material and value-chain integration assessment
  • Port and logistics infrastructure assessment
  • Labour ecosystem and workforce assessment
  • Industrial ecosystem and government incentive assessment
  • Location screening across potential West Bengal sites
  • Preliminary textile-park conceptualisation
Analytical Workstreams
01 — Market Intelligence Assessed global, regional and Indian MMF textile demand, supply dynamics, segmentation and growth opportunities.
02 — Product Opportunity Mapping Evaluated product families and export-oriented SKU opportunities based on demand, trade access, regulation and commercial considerations.
03 — Competitive Benchmarking Compared India's position with major Asian textile manufacturing and export hubs across value-chain integration and manufacturing capabilities.
04 — Location Assessment Screened candidate locations using road, rail, port access, industrial ecosystem and expansion-potential considerations.
05 — Infrastructure & Logistics Assessed port connectivity, cargo movement, multimodal logistics and infrastructure requirements of an export-oriented textile hub.
06 — Industrial Ecosystem Reviewed raw-material integration, workforce availability, industrial incentives, utilities and supporting infrastructure.
Key Strategic Questions Addressed
  • What MMF and apparel product segments offer meaningful opportunity?
  • Which export markets are relevant for the proposed ecosystem?
  • How does India compare with established Asian MMF textile hubs?
  • What value-chain capabilities can be developed locally?
  • Which locations offer suitable connectivity and expansion potential?
  • How can logistics and port infrastructure support exports?
  • What workforce and industrial ecosystem capabilities are available?
  • What government policies and incentives can support investment?
Business Application

The research was structured to support investment and pre-feasibility decision-making around an integrated MMF and apparel textile ecosystem, including product prioritisation, export-market assessment, location strategy, infrastructure requirements and preliminary park conceptualisation.

My Role

Senior Research Analyst — market research, market and trade analysis, competitive benchmarking, product/SKU opportunity assessment, company and industry research, location and ecosystem assessment, data validation, strategic synthesis and client-ready presentation development.
